---
title: My run streak rules
date: 2026-11-27
---
Been going for 900 days now and haven't written anything yet. So thought I'd just lay out the rules for my run streak. As a minimum, I follow Streak Runners International's definition:

"Run at least one mile (1.61 kilometres) within each calendar day."

That's pretty much it. Over the last year or so, I have bumped it to a minimum of 2km unless there are any rare exceptions, e.g. not feeling well or restricted with time or space. Like that time I had a stomach bug, or that time I ran 5 beers into a stag party.

I keep it simple; the most complex logistics have been long-haul flights, e.g. to Sydney, where I was forced to run in the departures terminal at Guangzhou airport. I have also chosen to run at 3 a.m. on days when I travel because, for example, a flight delay could mess up the streak.

But other than that, keep it simple. Run everyday.
